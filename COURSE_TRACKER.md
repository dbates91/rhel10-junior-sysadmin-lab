# Official Course Tracker

## Course 1 — Red Hat Junior Linux System Administrator Bootcamp

**Duration:** 12 weeks  
**Alignment:** RH124 → RH134 → RHCSA EX200  
**Current position:** Week 1, Lesson 2  
**Internal passing standard:** 85%  
**Advancement rule:** competency-based, not calendar-based

## Dual Graduation Objectives

### Track A — Junior Linux System Administrator Interview Readiness
Graduate able to:
- explain core Linux administration concepts clearly
- reason through realistic junior-level tickets
- investigate before changing a system
- justify administrative decisions
- verify outcomes and communicate them professionally
- walk through GitHub labs and troubleshooting evidence

### Track B — RHCSA EX200 Examination Readiness
Graduate able to:
- complete the current published RHEL 10 EX200 objectives without step-by-step assistance
- work accurately under timed, performance-based conditions
- verify actual system state rather than relying on command history
- make persistent configurations survive reboot where applicable
- pass three consecutive internal comprehensive practical mocks at 85% or higher

## Weekly Dual Assessment

### Track A — Interview
Evaluate:
- technical understanding
- troubleshooting method
- communication
- scenario reasoning
- ability to explain decisions

### Track B — RHCSA
Evaluate:
- command proficiency
- configuration accuracy
- independent completion
- troubleshooting
- persistence where applicable
- performance under time constraints

## Grading

- Guided and independent labs: 40%
- Troubleshooting exercises: 25%
- Practical examinations: 25%
- Technical explanations and documentation: 10%

## Mastery Rules

An RHCSA objective is not **Exam-ready** because a video was watched or a guided lab was completed.

Progression:

**Not started → Introduced → Guided lab completed → Independent lab completed → Practical assessment passed → Exam-ready**

Use **Needs remediation** whenever a previously attempted skill cannot be repeated accurately or independently.

## Mock Exam Readiness Standard

Before recommending the real RHCSA exam:

- score at least 85% on three consecutive comprehensive practical mock exams
- complete critical administration tasks successfully
- verify required persistent configuration after reboot
- remediate and reassess failed objective areas

This is the bootcamp's internal readiness standard, not Red Hat's official passing threshold.
